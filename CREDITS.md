## Scientific references

NASA ECLSS, NASA Space Food Systems, NASA fuel-cell references, and the Smithsonian
Apollo Astronaut Life article inform the optional explanations. Links and
simulation assumptions are in `README.md`.

The narrative, story asteroid and story radar telemetry are fictional game content.

The separate historical catalogue uses NASA NeoWs close-approach records.
`data/neows_2010.json` records the source, encounter dates and retrieval timestamp.
Greenhouse learning content is inspired by the CSA food-production toolkit;
growth speeds, nutritional effects and recovery efficiency are game assumptions,
not claimed CSA hardware performance.
